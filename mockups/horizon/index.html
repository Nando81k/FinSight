Horizon

Interactive iOS prototype · v1.4

A money app built around one question: when do you get where you're going? Everything on the phone is tappable, swipeable and scrubbable.

Gestures

  • Drag from the left edge of a pushed screen to go back
  • Press and drag across any chart to scrub values
  • Drag a sheet's grabber down to dismiss it
  • Swipe the welcome carousel and watch the sun rise
  • Tap the active tab to pop back to its root

All people, institutions, balances and prices are sample data for this prototype. Nothing connects to a real account.
